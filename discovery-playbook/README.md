# Discovery Playbook

A discovery call framework built from 7+ years of B2B selling - the questions that actually uncover budget, authority, and urgency.

## Structure
- Opening (3 min) - permission-based framing, agenda lock
- Current state (15 min) - "walk me through how you handle X today" - workflow, pain, cost of inaction
- Impact (10 min) - quantify: what does this problem cost per month/quarter?
- Decision process (10 min) - who decides, what do they care about, what kills deals here
- Close (5 min) - mutual action plan with dates

## What good sounds like
Each section includes the follow-up questions that separate surface answers from real intel, plus the red flags that mean the deal is not real.
