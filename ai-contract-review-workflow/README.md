# AI Contract Review Workflow

An AI-assisted contract review method with strict guardrails - speed without losing the audit trail.

## The workflow
1. Intake - contract/SOW arrives
2. Strip ALL PII and proprietary identifiers before anything touches AI
3. AI first pass - clause extraction + red-flag synthesis into a standardized summary
4. Human validation - every AI claim checked against the rate card / source record (zero-defect audit trail)
5. Triage - clean / negotiate / escalate
6. Documented decision

## Guardrails
- No PII ever enters an AI tool
- No AI output treated as final - always validated
- Attorney review for legal work product
