# Outbound Prospecting Playbook

A field-tested outbound system from a top 2% performer - the cadence, channel mix, and messaging that actually books meetings.

## The system
- Ideal customer profile - firmographics + trigger events that signal buying intent
- Cadence - 8 touches over 21 days: call, email, LinkedIn, call, email, video, call, breakup
- Channel mix - phone first (highest connect-to-meeting rate), email for nurturing, LinkedIn for warming
- Messaging - personalization at the trigger level, one clear pain hypothesis per touch, every touch earns the next

## What good looks like
Reply-rate benchmarks per channel, the follow-up discipline that separates pros from quitters, and the breakup email that resurrects dead threads.
